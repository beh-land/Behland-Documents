# BehLand DAO Governance Framework

## Progressive Governance

BehLand follows a gradual transition toward community governance:

1. Central Management
2. Internal Delegation
3. Community Participation
4. Light DAO
5. Expanded Community Governance

## Governance Philosophy

Voting power is based on meaningful contribution, history, participation, and ecosystem reputation — not only token ownership.

## Governance Layers

### Active Learners
Can participate in surveys and provide proposals.

### Specialists and Contributors
Participate in educational standards and ecosystem improvement.

### BehLand Council
Reviews important decisions and protects ecosystem culture.

## Governance Member Requirements

Entry requires demonstrated long-term contribution and approval according to governance rules.

Detailed execution rules will evolve with ecosystem maturity.
