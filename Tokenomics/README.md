# BEHT Tokenomics Overview

## Overview

BEHT is the utility token of the BehLand ecosystem. It is designed to support ecosystem interactions, services, and long-term participation. BEHT is not a promise of profit, fixed return, or guaranteed appreciation.

## Supply

Total supply: **10,000,000 BEHT**

## Allocation

| Category | Share |
|---|---:|
| Learning Pool | 35% |
| Ecosystem Development | 25% |
| Founder | 10% |
| Marketing & Community | 10% |
| Presale | 10% |
| Main Managers | 5% |
| Executive Team | 5% |

## Utility

BEHT can be used for:
- Ecosystem services
- VIP membership economic participation
- NTT registration mechanisms
- Speed Mechanism costs
- Marketplace and ecosystem interactions

## Economic Principles

- Education remains free.
- Money cannot buy skills, PoHW, or educational status.
- Reward systems depend on real contribution, PoHW, and ecosystem conditions.

For detailed economics, refer to the official LitePaper V3.
