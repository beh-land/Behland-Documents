# BehLand Characters

## Overview

Characters represent human behaviors and learning personalities inside the BehLand ecosystem. They are not only mascots; they reflect different aspects of human growth.

## Main Characters

- Behcow — Learning and companionship
- Behdonk — Courage and risk-taking
- Behsheep — Caution and evaluation
- Behbull — Knowledge and discipline
- Behbear — Experience and control

## Secondary Characters

- Behfox — Curiosity and clever exploration
- Behpig — Enjoyment and reward motivation

Characters can evolve through real learning progress, missions, and achievements. Growth cannot be purchased.
